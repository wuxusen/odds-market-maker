Autonomous market making · live odds · paper trading

An autonomous in-play odds market maker, running on real TxODDS TxLINE World Cup data

It ingests a real in-play 1X2 odds line, prices a de-vigged consensus fair value, quotes two-sided with inventory skew and hard exposure caps, and cancels everything the instant a goal, red card, feed stall or latency spike hits. Execution is paper-only, written to a hash-chained audit ledger that is anchored on-chain.

What you're looking at

Real TxLINE odds

The first segment replays a captured, real TxODDS TxLINE in-play line for Portugal v Croatia. Every fair value, quote, paper fill and PnL figure is what the engine produced from that feed — nothing hand-authored.

Circuit breaker on real goals

Both breaker trips you see are driven by genuine goal-time line dislocations in the feed — a goal against the favourite, then the equaliser. The engine flattens and stops quoting through the discontinuity, then resumes.

Paper execution, real audit trail

All trading is paper-only. Every fill and settlement is appended to a SHA-256 hash-chained ledger, so the track record is tamper-evident — you can't rewrite a fill without breaking the chain.

On-chain anchor (verify it yourself)

The ledger's rolling head hash is anchored to Solana devnet in a real Memo transaction. Decode the memo and it matches the head byte-for-byte — open the transaction on Solana Explorer →

The pipeline

01 · FEED
Ingest

Read the in-play 1X2 line from TxODDS TxLINE (SSE/REST), normalise per book.

02 · PRICE
De-vig & quote

Strip the overround to a consensus fair value, then quote two-sided with inventory skew.

03 · GUARD
Circuit breaker

Goal, red card, feed stall or latency spike → cancel everything, flatten, pause.

04 · PROVE
Ledger & anchor

Hash-chain every fill; periodically anchor the chain head on Solana devnet.
